Questions

Who is Promethia for?

Students in Grades 5–12 today, and all grades later. We work through schools in Bangalore.

Is it online?

No. Promethia is offline and hands-on. Sessions happen in person at school, in small cohorts.

What happens in a session?

About 5 minutes to learn a skill, 5 minutes on the day's topic, then 25–30 minutes hands-on or on stage. Sessions run 30–60 minutes, once or twice a week.

Who is Promo?

Promo is our torch-flame guide. Promo cheers kids on and asks good questions, but never gives away the answer.

What do parents get?

A portfolio page with session recordings, what your child is good at, their interests, what they can achieve and how to get better. See a sample.

How can our school join?

Email founder@promethia.live and we'll set up a chat.
